# Audio

## Game audio

The PC's sound plays through the phone's speaker, or headphones and AirPods when they're connected. On a Mac, Steam needs its streaming audio driver (see [Streaming from a Mac](pcs-and-setup.md#streaming-from-a-mac)).

## Voice chat

Settings › Audio › **Microphone**. Off by default, and it applies from the next time you connect.

Your voice goes to the PC, where games, Discord and Steam hear it as the PC's microphone. The first time, iOS asks to let iPlayPC use the microphone. If you said no, turn it on in the iOS Settings app under **Apps › iPlayPC**.

- **Mute:** while streaming, tap the mic button in the bottom-left corner, or use **Mute microphone** in the stream menu.
- **Echo:** on the phone's speaker, echo cancellation keeps the game audio out of your mic, so nobody hears it back.
- **Paused:** the mic is silent while the stream is paused, for example when you fold an iPhone Duo or switch apps.
