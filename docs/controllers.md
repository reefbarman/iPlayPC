# Controllers

## How your controller appears on the PC

Your controller shows up on the PC as a real gamepad, so Steam Input and games show the right buttons:

- **PlayStation controllers** (DualSense, DualShock 4) appear as a DualSense, with the touchpad, gyro, rumble and lightbar. Turn on **Steam › Settings › Controller › PlayStation Controller Support** on the PC to get the extras.
- **Everything else** (Xbox, Backbone and other MFi controllers), and the on-screen controls, appear as an Xbox controller with rumble.
- Controllers without motion sensors (such as a Backbone) use the phone's gyro.
- Switching controllers mid-game swaps what the PC sees.

With a DualSense or DualShock 4 connected, the app names buttons the PlayStation way (Cross, Circle, L2 and so on).

Hold **View + Menu** for a second during a stream to open the stream menu.

## The Xbox button

The controller's Xbox or PS button goes to Steam, which opens the Steam overlay or Big Picture. To press it without a controller, tap the top-left corner of the stream, then the Xbox logo.

## Rumble

Settings › Controller › Vibration.

- **Rumble:** turns all vibration on or off. Games rumble the controller, or the phone when the controller has no motors. On by default.
- **Controller vibration:** Light, Medium or Full (the default). How strongly a controller's motors play rumble.
- **Rumble updates:** how many vibration changes per second go to a wireless controller, from 10 to Unlimited (default 25). They share the controller's Bluetooth connection with your button presses, so too many can make the controls stop responding for a moment during heavy vibration. Lower it if that happens, raise it if vibration feels less detailed.
- **Phone haptics:** Off, Light or Full (the default). How strongly the phone itself vibrates, which it does with virtual controls or a controller without motors. Not shown on iPads, which have no vibration motor.

## Gyro aiming

Settings › Controller › Gyro aiming. Off by default.

Turns the controller's motion (or the phone's, if the controller has none) into right stick movement, on top of the stick itself. Use it **Always**, or only **While holding LT** or **While holding RB**.

- **Look left/right by:** Turn and tilt (the default), Turn, or Tilt like a steering wheel.
- **Gyro sensitivity:** how far the camera moves for a given turn.
- **Up/down speed:** makes looking up and down faster than left and right, as most games turn slower vertically.
- **Invert gyro up/down.**
- **Game dead zone:** how much stick movement the game ignores. Raise it until small, slow movements move the camera, and lower it if the camera creeps when you hold still.
- **Dead zone per direction:** pushes left/right and up/down past the dead zone separately, so the small part of a diagonal correction isn't lost.
- **Fine aim boost:** undoes games slowing down small stick movements. Raise it if small corrections feel sluggish, lower it if they overshoot. 1 turns it off.

Steam Input on the PC can also use a DualSense's own gyro, independently of this setting.

## Controller Test

**Settings › Controller › Controller Test**.

A 3D controller lights up each button, stick and trigger as the game gets it, turns with the controller's gyro, and tries rumble. Use it to check a controller before blaming the game.
