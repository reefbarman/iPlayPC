# Touch and virtual controls

## Virtual controls

Settings › Virtual controls.

An Xbox layout with coloured A, B, X and Y buttons. The PC sees an Xbox controller.

- **Show virtual controls:** Auto (the default) shows them only when no supported controller is connected. Always show and Never show ignore the controller. With no controller connected, iPlayPC asks before the stream starts unless this is Always show (see [Starting without a controller](controllers.md#starting-without-a-controller)).
- **Size:** Small, Medium (the default) or Large.
- **Opacity:** from 20% to 100%.
- **Fixed sticks:** sticks normally start wherever your thumb lands in the lower half. Fixed sticks stay in one spot on each side instead, and a touch near one moves it straight away.

## Touch as a mouse

Settings › Virtual controls › **Touch as mouse**. On by default.

For the desktop, launchers and menus that want a pointer.

- **With a controller:** tap the picture to click exactly there, and drag to drag.
- **With virtual controls showing:** the top half of the screen works like a laptop trackpad. Drag to move the pointer, tap to click, and tap then drag to hold the button.
- **Either way:** press and hold, or tap with two fingers, to right-click. Drag with two fingers to scroll.

## Typing

Tap the screen with three fingers to open the iPhone keyboard. See [Typing with the keyboard](stream-screen.md#typing-with-the-keyboard).
